CrowdShield

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

CrowdShield ("we", "our", "the platform") is an AI-powered early warning system designed to monitor, predict, and help prevent crowd congestion and stampede risks at venues and campuses. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the CrowdShield Citizen Portal and Admin Command Dashboard, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

  • Hazard Reports: When you submit a report through the Citizen Portal, we collect the category, description, location/coordinates, and any optional media (photos or video) you choose to attach.
  • Location Data: To route you along safe evacuation paths and to associate hazard reports with the correct venue zone, we collect approximate or precise device location while you use Safe Exit Maps or submit a report.
  • Telemetry & Zone Data: The platform processes aggregated crowd density, flow rate, and risk-score telemetry derived from venue sensors and CCTV feeds. This data is generally analyzed at the zone level, not tied to a specific individual.
  • CCTV & Camera Feeds: Venue-operated camera feeds supply bounding-box data to our machine learning risk engine for the purpose of estimating crowd density and movement patterns.
  • Account & Device Information: Basic session data, device type, and view-mode preferences (e.g., Citizen vs. Admin) may be stored locally to preserve your settings across visits.
  • Communications: If you contact support or interact with in-app notifications, we may retain a record of that communication.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To detect, predict, and respond to crowd congestion and stampede risks in real time.
  • To broadcast safety alerts, evacuation routing, and multilingual instructions to affected users via WebSocket push and text-to-speech notifications.
  • To relay citizen hazard reports to venue administrators for triage in the Alerts Sentinel dashboard.
  • To train and improve our predictive risk-scoring and pathfinding models.
  • To maintain system audit logs for safety, accountability, and incident review.

3. How Information Is Shared

Hazard reports and relevant location data are shared with authorized venue administrators and safety personnel so they can coordinate an appropriate response. Aggregated or de-identified telemetry may be used for analytics and system improvement. We do not sell personal information to third parties. Data may be disclosed where required by law or to protect the safety of individuals during an active emergency.

4. Data Storage & Security

Data submitted through CrowdShield is stored in our managed cloud database and accessed through encrypted, access-controlled application services. Real-time data in transit between clients and the backend is transmitted over secured WebSocket and HTTPS connections. While we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, no system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

Incident reports and audit logs are retained for as long as necessary to support venue safety operations, incident investigation, and applicable legal or regulatory requirements. Aggregated telemetry used for model training may be retained in de-identified form for longer periods.

6. Your Choices

  • You may decline to attach media or precise location to a hazard report, though this may reduce the usefulness of the report.
  • You may disable location permissions in your device settings; some features, such as Safe Exit Maps, may not function correctly without it.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to safety and legal obligations, by contacting the venue administrator or platform operator.

7. Children's Privacy

CrowdShield is intended for general venue safety use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from young children without appropriate consent where required by law.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. Material changes will be indicated by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the venue operator or system administrator responsible for your CrowdShield deployment.
